## Hook (verbatim)
"If you've ever read a LinkedIn post that went out under your name and thought 'that doesn't sound like me' — you don't have a writing problem. You have a capture problem. In the next few minutes I'm going to show you the actual 10-minute weekly habit that replaced a $750-a-month ghostwriter, and prove every post it produces traces back to something I actually said that week — not a writer's memory of a call from three months ago."

## Structure
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Hook — the "doesn't sound like me" problem, named immediately |
| 0:15–1:00 | Why ghostwriting fails structurally: interview once, write from memory forever |
| 1:00–2:30 | Show the real weekly voice-note — record one live, unedited |
| 2:30–5:00 | Show that voice note turned into 3 real draft posts, live, side by side with the audio |
| 5:00–6:30 | Read 1 finished post next to the voice-note line it came from — prove the traceability claim |
| 6:30–7:30 | The algorithm math: why 1x/month posting resets you and 2-5x/week compounds |
| 7:30–8:00 | CTA to the Voice-Capture Starter Kit |

## On-screen proof (must be real, not summarized)
- An actual voice memo recording, played or transcribed on screen, not described after the fact.
- The real draft document showing the voice note transcript next to the three generated posts.
- At least one finished post with the specific line from the voice note it was built from highlighted — the traceability claim has to be checkable by the viewer, not asserted.

## Cut list
- No explanation of what AI or an LLM is.
- No stock "ghostwriter is bad" claim without naming the specific mechanism failure (interview-once-write-forever).
- No slide listing "benefits of consistency" — show the algorithm math as a stated fact, don't dwell on it as a selling point.
- No gate before the proof — the voice note, the drafts, and the traceability check all happen before any mention of the Starter Kit.
