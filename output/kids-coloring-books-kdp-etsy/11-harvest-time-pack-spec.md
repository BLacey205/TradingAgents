## Content rules for the drawings

Use the workflow doc's checks, run per page:
1. A 5-year-old could name the main subject at a glance.
2. The action and setting match the scene spec; every "looks like the real thing" feature is visible.
3. Nothing from the MUST NOT INCLUDE list appears (no text, no border or frame, no dashed lines, no solid black fills except small eyes or pupils).
4. Pure black solid lines on white, closed shapes white inside, the thick-line tier for ages 4-5.
5. Upscaled to at least 2250 x 3000 px and still pure black and white.
6. Matches the line thickness and style of Pack 1 so the packs look like one set. Generate against an approved page and say "match the line thickness and drawing style of the attached image exactly."

If a page still fails after 3 tries, rewrite its scene spec with more specific features rather than keep regenerating.

## Build steps

1. Confirm pages 21-30 are Done in the tracker (status, file names above).
2. Make the title page (pack name), the "belongs to" page and the certificate in Canva, matching the Pack 1 layouts.
3. Assemble the 13 pages in the order above, 0.5 in margins, 300 DPI, no blank backs.
4. Export as `Farm-Pack3-Pages21-30.pdf`; check size under 20 MB.
5. Print it at home on US Letter at 100%, and test A4 with "Fit to page".
6. Make listing photos: the cover first, then an all-drawings grid, a flat-lay mockup and one page shown large (the workflow doc's order). Only real prints in the flat-lay.
7. Run the 3-parent feedback from Day 6 (ask what they'd change; don't ask them to buy).

## What not to claim

- No reviews, ratings, sales counts, "best seller", "trending" or "parent favorite".
- No "ten fall pages" or "ten pages" (it's 13 pages with 10 drawings, harvest-season rather than all-fall).
- No "kids love it", "keeps kids busy", learning or fine-motor claims, or "better than free".
- No discount, countdown or "limited time". The seasonal reason to buy now is stated plainly as the harvest theme; don't invent urgency.
