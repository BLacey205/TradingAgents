## Rules every email follows

- No price (say "the price is on the listing"), discount, countdown, urgency, "limited time" or launch special.
- No reviews, ratings, sales counts, "best seller", parent quotes or income claims. No review requests or incentives.
- No per-order time, no seller earnings talk, and no mention of the multi-name Farm Party Set (it is locked).
- Turnaround appears only as "within [turnaround as stated on the live listing]". It must match the live Etsy listing and be a promise you can keep.
- Not "instant download": the file is made by hand after the spelling is confirmed.
- AI-generated line art is disclosed wherever the book is pitched.
- Every email carries the sender block and a working unsubscribe link. All purchases happen on Etsy.
- Timing (adjust freely): Email 2 on Day 2, Email 3 on Day 4, Email 4 on Day 7, Email 5 on Day 10.

## Email 2 — Day 2

- Subject: The one printing mistake that cuts off the page
- Preview text: It's almost always one setting.
- Body (plain text, copy as is):

  Hi [first name or "there"],

  If the free cow page printed cut off, shrunken, or with the edges missing, it's almost always one printer setting, and it's quick to fix.

  1. Open the print dialog and set the scale to "Actual size" or 100%. Turn off "Fit to page" if it's on.
  2. On US Letter paper, keep the margins as they are. The page is built with 0.5 in margins so nothing is cut off at 100%.
  3. On A4 paper, do the opposite: choose "Fit to page" so the whole drawing fits.
  4. Print on regular printer paper. If you're coloring with markers, put a scrap sheet underneath. That's a tip, not a guarantee, because paper weight varies.

  If it still looks off, hit reply with what you see and I'll take a look.

  And if you've already printed it: how did it come out? A photo or a single line is plenty. No pressure.

  [Your first name]
  [Shop name]

- Link: none. (CTA is an optional reply.) If reply volume gets unmanageable, drop the reply ask.

## Check before sending (none confirmed here)

1. CAN-SPAM: accurate From name and reply address, a valid physical postal address, a working unsubscribe link, no purchased lists, and subject lines that match the content. Emails 1-5 are commercial messages. Stricter consent rules (GDPR, PECR, CASL) apply if any traffic comes from the EU, UK or Canada; not assessed here.
2. Etsy: whether linking to Etsy listings from an email is fine (it should be), whether mentioning the spelling-confirmation flow matches how Etsy messages and digital-order delivery actually work, and the AI-disclosure wording. Do not use Etsy buyer data for this list, and offer no discounts, gifts or free files in exchange for reviews.
3. Turnaround: "[turnaround as stated on the live listing]" must be filled with the exact wording on the listing, and you must be able to keep it. Time your first 3 orders first.
4. Email 3 images: the sample name must be labeled as a sample; the printed-and-colored page must be real or left out; the spelling message stays labeled SAMPLE unless a real buyer gives permission.
5. Email 4's optional P.S.: only while Harvest Time is live and in season. Fix the Harvest Time page count first (02-offer.md says 13 pages; the workflow doc lists 10 drawings), though this email doesn't state a count.
6. After Email 5, no further promotional emails unless you decide on a separate, disclosed cadence. Reader replies about multiple names are logged but don't count toward the Farm Party Set unlock.
7. Track sign-ups, Email 1 downloads, clicks to each Etsy listing and Etsy's own listing views by source. Set your pass/fail thresholds before launch; none are sourced here.
