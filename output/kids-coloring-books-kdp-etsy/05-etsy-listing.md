## Verify before publishing (none confirmed here)

1. Etsy category and whether a made-to-order digital file is delivered by order message or file upload, and how that interacts with the Etsy processing-time setting for digital items.
2. Etsy's current AI-disclosure setting and wording requirements.
3. That "within 48 hours" is a promise you can keep; time your first 3 orders. Do not publish a faster claim.
4. The title, tags and price against a logged-out Etsy search for "personalized farm coloring book" and "personalized coloring book with name" (record listing counts, prices, review counts and top 3 sellers' review text). That check can overturn this angle.
5. Fee math and your listing fee in Etsy's current fee page; the net figures in 02-offer.md are from memory.
6. Whether Etsy allows the "no returns" wording, and any region-specific consumer rights.
7. That the sample name, the SAMPLE spelling message and the printed-page photo are real and correctly labeled before upload.
