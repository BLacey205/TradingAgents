## Notes and checks

- Link: only the PDF download. Nothing links to Etsy or to a price. The pitch comes in emails 3 and 5.
- "Hand it over" and "chunky crayons or markers" are tips, not claims that kids will like it or that markers won't bleed.
- The reply ask collects real buyer language for the Day 6 conversations. Don't promise a response time you can't keep. If volume becomes unmanageable, drop the question.
- If the cow page keeps the blank name banner (the spec choice in 04-conversion.md), the email says "no name on it" and doesn't promise a free personalized page. Remove the banner area if it reads as a free personalized page.
- Don't add Etsy buyers to this list. Members come only from the sign-up form.

## Verify before sending (none confirmed here)

1. CAN-SPAM: accurate From name and reply address, a valid physical postal address, a working one-click unsubscribe link, and a subject that matches the content. If any EU, UK or Canada traffic comes from Pinterest, stricter consent rules (GDPR, PECR, CASL) apply and aren't assessed here.
2. MailerLite: free-plan limits, whether automations and landing pages are included, how the PDF is delivered (link vs attachment), and its confirmation and sending-address requirements.
3. The AI-generated art disclosure matches the Etsy listing wording and Etsy's current rules.
4. The PDF itself: US Letter, 0.5 in margins, 300 DPI, one-sided, opens and prints correctly at 100% and on A4.
5. The reply-to inbox exists and you can read it before you launch.
