## Lead magnet

Name: **Free Farm Cow Page — Print Test** (generic, no child's name; the real cow page from Pack 1 "Farm Favorites").

Contents (one component, kept small on purpose):
1. `farm-cow-page.pdf` — the unretouched cow drawing from Pack 1, US Letter, 0.5 in margins, 300 DPI, very thick outlines, one-sided. The banner area where a name goes in the paid book is left blank, so the buyer sees where the name would sit. This is the same file quality as the paid pages; no lower-quality sample. (Spec choice to confirm: blank banner on, default. If it reads as "a free personalized page", remove it, since the CTA must not promise a free personalized page.)
2. No second file. Print tips go in email 1 and 2 as text, not a separate document, so there is nothing extra to build.

Framing: honest framing is "a test print", not "infrastructure". The offer's mechanism (the Name-Swap Farm Master File) runs on the seller's side, so there is nothing for the buyer to install. The magnet solves "I'm not sure this art style is right for my kid" by letting them print one page and see the line thickness for themselves. Do not call it a toolkit or system. Do not claim kids will love it or that it beats free pages.

AI disclosure: the welcome email and the page footer state that the line art is AI-generated, matching the Etsy listing disclosure (see compliance checklist).

## Delivery mechanics

Platform: MailerLite free plan (named in the offer; confirm current free-plan limits, whether landing pages and automations are included, and the sending-address and double opt-in requirements on their pricing and terms pages. Not verified here).

Entry point: a MailerLite sign-up page linked from the Pinterest pin description or profile. The Etsy listing and the Etsy video must NOT link to it (see Etsy checks). Form fields: email only. No child's name, age or birthdate (the audience is parents and gift-givers, and collecting children's data adds COPPA risk for no benefit).

The instant they opt in:
1. Confirmation step (double opt-in if MailerLite's setup requires or recommends it for your sending region; it also helps with consent records).
2. Immediately after confirming, Email 1 is sent with the PDF attached or linked.
3. Immediate first step (3 minutes): "Print page 1 on plain paper at 100% / Actual size (US Letter). On A4, choose Fit to page. Hand it over with chunky crayons or markers." That is the whole setup.

What they do not get: no name personalization, no discount, no urgency, no price. Purchase always happens on Etsy.

Sender block on every email (placeholders to fill): [Shop name], [real contact email for replies], [valid postal address], a working one-click unsubscribe link.

## Email sequence

Suggested timing (adjust freely): Day 0, Day 2, Day 4, Day 7, Day 10. Plain text or very light HTML; each email has one link or one reply ask. Directions only, not final copy. [bracketed] items are placeholders.

1. **Subject:** "Your free farm cow page (print it first)" — **Direction:** Deliver the PDF. Say what it is: one real page from the farm book, generic, no name on it. Give the 3-minute step: print at 100% (A4: Fit to page), use chunky crayons or markers, put a spare sheet under it. State plainly that the art is AI-generated and disclosed on the listings. Ask one low-pressure question: reply and tell me who you'd print it for (this collects real buyer language for the Day 6 research; do not promise a response time you cannot keep). No sales pitch. — **CTA:** the PDF download link.

2. **Subject:** "The one printing mistake that cuts off the page" — **Direction:** Fix the #1 setup mistake: printer scaling. If the page prints cut off or tiny, it is almost always scale settings: pick Actual size / 100% on US Letter, or Fit to page on A4, and keep the 0.5 in margins. Add the practical tip of a scrap sheet under the page when using markers (stated as a tip, not as a guarantee that markers will not bleed; paper weight varies). Do not claim thick paper fixes it. — **CTA:** reply with a photo or one line on how the print came out (optional; no link). Optional: skip the reply ask if volume becomes unmanageable.

3. **Subject:** "What a finished name-on-every-page book looks like" — **Direction:** Show what "working" looks like using only real or clearly labeled material: (a) the cover and 3-4 drawing pages with a labeled sample name (e.g. "Mason", marked "sample name"); (b) the spelling check, shown as a labeled sample (below); (c) a real printed and colored page only if one exists, captioned honestly as colored by an adult if no child has done it, otherwise leave it out. Say you confirm the spelling by message before sending the file, and fix a seller typo free. State honestly that the shop is new and has no reviews yet, so the preview pages are the thing to judge by (owner decision; keep it plain, no fake proof, no review incentives or discounts). — **CTA:** link to the personalized book listing on Etsy ("see the sample pages"), view only.

## Handoff into the paid rungs

- Every purchase happens on Etsy. Emails link only to Etsy listings. Never take orders, names or payment by email or by a link to another platform.
- The in-video CTA now says "order on Etsy and add the name at checkout", which routes names to the Etsy personalization box and Etsy messages, not email.
- Rung order in the funnel: free cow page (email 1) → personalized book (emails 3 and 5). The Harvest Time entry pack is a separate seasonal side link in email 4 only, not a required step.
- Do not add Etsy buyers' emails or names to the list. List members come only from the sign-up form. Do not use Etsy order or message data for marketing (see compliance).
- After email 5, no further promotional emails unless you decide on a separate, disclosed cadence. Reader replies about multiple names are logged but do not count toward the party-set unlock (see below).

## Sales path logic

- Lead magnet solves: "I'm not sure this art style, with these thick outlines, is right for my kid", answered by printing one free generic page before buying anything.
- Mid-ticket solves: "I want a gift made for one specific child, with their name in it", the one thing generic packs and free pages cannot do (the page content is the same kind; the name and the gift framing are the whole bet).
- Premium solves: "I need this for a whole birthday party, not one kid." LOCKED. Build and list only when both conditions hold: at least 10 strangers' sales of the single book, and at least one buyer has asked for multiple names. A reader's email reply is not a buyer asking, so it does not meet the condition. No party-demand evidence exists yet; the idea is a hypothesis from the research's "farm-themed party" occasion. Until unlocked it is absent from emails, pins, listings and video.

## Compliance checks (verify before launch; none confirmed here)

Etsy (read the current Seller Policy, Creativity Standards and the rules on communication and off-Etsy marketing; I could not confirm wording):
1. Are links to external sites allowed in listing descriptions, listing videos and shop pages? Assume no and keep the sign-up link on Pinterest only. The video end-card and CTA now say the free page is on Pinterest (not "from my shop"), since it lives off Etsy; still confirm Etsy allows mentioning an off-Etsy page in listing video at all, and drop the free-page line from the Etsy cut if not.
2. Etsy's rules on using buyer information (names, emails, messages) for marketing. Assume you may not add buyers to the list without separate consent. This plan does not.
3. Whether a free-download lead magnet can be delivered off-Etsy to Etsy-originated traffic, and whether Etsy permits steering buyers off the platform for anything beyond that. The paid rungs stay entirely on Etsy.
4. Reviews: Etsy forbids incentivized or gated review requests. The sequence offers no discount, gift or free file for a review.
5. AI-generated art disclosure setting on every listing, matching the statement in the emails (a requirement per the offer file; confirm current Etsy rules).
6. Personalization and message handling: whether "confirm the spelling by message" before delivery is allowed and how it interacts with Etsy's order and delivery-time settings.

CAN-SPAM (US; confirm current FTC guidance, and have counsel check if you want certainty):
1. Accurate From name and reply address; subject lines that match the content (the five above are written to).
2. A valid physical postal address in every email.
3. A clear, working unsubscribe link in every email; honor opt-outs promptly (the rule says within 10 business days; confirm).
4. Do not buy or rent lists. Only people who opted in via the form.
5. Emails are commercial (they promote a paid listing), so the commercial-message rules apply to emails 1-5, including email 1.
6. If any EU/UK/Canada traffic is possible from Pinterest, those regions have stricter consent rules (GDPR/PECR/CASL). Not assessed here; double opt-in and a clear consent line on the form are the safer default.
7. No data collection about children; the form asks for an email only.

## Experiment tracking (numbers to set yourself)

- Track: sign-ups, email 1 downloads, clicks to each Etsy listing, and Etsy's own listing views by traffic source. Do not conclude the magnet works from sign-ups alone; the test is whether any listing sales trace to the sequence.
- Pre-register your thresholds before launch. If sign-ups come but no clicks reach the listing, test the email 3 and 5 framing (name versus occasion) before changing the art, consistent with the content file.
- Real parent language from replies (emails 1, 2, 4) should replace the seller-side phrases in the listing and emails after the Day 6 conversations.
