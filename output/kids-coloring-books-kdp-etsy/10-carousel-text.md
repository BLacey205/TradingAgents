# 5-Slide Carousel Text — Personalized Farm Coloring Book

Built from the still-image carousel in 03-content.md, matching listing photos 1-5 in 05-etsy-listing.md and the static pins in 06-pinterest-pins.md. Use it for the Etsy listing's first five photos and as a static pin set. Format: 1:1 or 4:5 for Etsy (check Etsy's current photo specs), 2:3 for Pinterest pins. Big text, few words, readable on a phone.

## Rules for every slide

- A sample name ("Mason") must be labeled "sample name" in small print wherever it appears. It must never read as a customer.
- No price, discount, "limited time" or urgency.
- No reviews, ratings, sales counts, "best seller", "trending", "parent favorite" or parent quotes.
- No "instant download" (the file is made by hand after the spelling is confirmed).
- No per-order time, no learning or fine-motor claims, no competitor comparisons, no party-set or multi-name mention.
- The AI-generated line art is disclosed on the listing (slide 5 repeats it in small print; Etsy's AI-disclosure setting stays on).
- Real photos only where a slide says "real".

## Verify before publishing (none confirmed here)

1. Etsy's current photo size and text-overlay guidance and Pinterest's pin size guidance.
2. The sample name and the sample message are labeled as samples, and nothing implies a real customer, review or sale.
3. Slide 5 uses a real photo, with an honest caption about who colored it.
4. The turnaround in slide 4 matches the live listing and is a promise you can keep (time your first 3 orders).
5. The AI-disclosure setting is on in the Etsy listing, and Pinterest's AI-content rules (including any "AI modified" label) are followed on the pins.
6. Thumbnail text and keywords are unverified. Pick the headline for slide 1 by testing, not assumption.
