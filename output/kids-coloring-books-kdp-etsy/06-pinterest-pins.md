## Posting rhythm (a starting plan, not a benchmark)

- Pin A1 and A3 (video) first, then A2/A4/A5 spaced across the following weeks. Re-pin the same creative to a second board rather than inventing new copy.
- Keep B1 on its own schedule and take it down or let it age out after late November.
- Judge by the first 30 days of impressions and outbound clicks, matching the offer's Test 2 window. If pins get views but no clicks, test gift/occasion copy (A2, A5) against name copy (A1, A3) before changing the art. Set your own pass/fail numbers before posting; none are sourced here.

## Rules these pins follow

- No price, discount, countdown, "limited time" or "launch special".
- No reviews, ratings, sales counts, "best seller", "trending" or "parent favorite".
- Not "instant download": the file is made by hand after the spelling is confirmed.
- No per-order time, no seller earnings talk, no multi-name or party-set mention (that tier is locked).
- No learning or fine-motor claims.
- The sample name ("Mason") is always a sample and must never read as a customer.
- Real photos only for the printed-and-colored beat.

## Verify before posting (none confirmed here)

1. Pinterest's current character limits and its policy on AI-generated imagery or disclosure labels. If Pinterest offers an "AI modified" label, use it on pins that show the AI line art.
2. Pinterest's rules on linking to Etsy and to an email sign-up page, and on promoting a lead magnet.
3. The Harvest Time pack's page count: 02-offer.md says a 13-page pack, while the workflow doc lists 10 drawings (pages 21-30). B1 says "ten" because the ten drawings are listed; correct it to match the real file.
4. That "within 48 hours" and "order the night before" in A2 match the live Etsy listing and a turnaround you can keep.
5. The keyword stems (board names, titles) against Pinterest search suggestions; they come from competitor listings and are unverified.
6. The free-page pin's sign-up page exists, matches the AI disclosure, and carries the sender block and unsubscribe link per CAN-SPAM (see 04-conversion.md).
