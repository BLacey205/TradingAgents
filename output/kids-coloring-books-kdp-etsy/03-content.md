# Content Angle — Personalized farm coloring book (Etsy printable, child's name on cover and every page)

Built on 01-research.md and 02-offer.md (both 2026-10-01).

## Channel decision and honesty rules

Channels are Pinterest preview pins and Etsy listing photos/short-form video, not long-form YouTube. So the "piece" is a 20-25 second vertical video, reused as (a) the Etsy listing video, (b) a Pinterest video pin, and (c) a 5-slide still carousel built from the same frames for the Etsy listing photos and static pins. The "title" is the Pinterest pin title and Etsy listing title; the "hook" is the on-screen text plus first-line voiceover (or captions only, since Etsy video is often watched muted).

The angle: **one name, swapped live, into a finished farm book.** The mechanism (Name-Swap Farm Master File) is the visible proof. The buyer is the gift-giver, so the voice stays on gift/occasion and screen-free activity language.

Claims that must NOT appear anywhere in this content (all unverified in the research/offer):
- Any sales count, review count, "best seller," "loved by parents," "parent-approved," or quotes from parents. No parent quotes exist.
- "Ready in 15 minutes" or any per-order time. That figure is an unmeasured estimate, and it is a seller-side figure the buyer does not need. The buyer-facing promise is the offer's 48-hour delivery commitment (aim for 24), which is a service promise, not proof.
- Prices in video or on thumbnails. Prices ($7.99 launch, $9.99 later, $24.99 party tier) are untested estimates; Etsy shows the live price, so the content never quotes one. The Farm Party Set is not shown at all until the offer's unlock conditions are met.
- "Instant download." The file is made by hand after the name is confirmed.
- That kids love it, that it keeps kids busy for X minutes, or that it works better than free pages.
- Hiding the AI line art. Disclosure stays on the listing; the video does not need to dwell on it but must not imply hand-drawing.

## Hook (verbatim)

Format: first 3 seconds on-screen text, then voiceover/caption through second 15.

On-screen text, 0:00-0:03: "Free farm pages. Nobody's name on them."

Voiceover/captions:
"Free farm coloring pages are everywhere, and none of them say who they're for. [Cut to cover with sample name 'Mason' appearing.] This one says 'This book belongs to Mason', and so does every page after it. Watch the name go in. [Cut to Canva master file, name typed, banner updates on every page.] You send a name, I confirm the spelling, and the PDF comes to you to print for the birthday bag, the farm party, or the basket."

Why this hook: it names the gap in the research (what they do now: grab free or $1.50-$3 generic pages with no name or gift feel; what the product adds: a finished farm book made for one child). It states what they will have: a printable book with that child's name on the cover and every page. It does not manufacture urgency, since the research found cost of inaction weak; the only trigger it points to is the gift occasion.

## On-screen proof (must be real, not summarized)

1. **The actual name swap in the real Canva master file**, screen-recorded in one take: type the name once, show the cover, belongs-to page, and drawing banners changing. No pre-baked cuts between "before" and "after."
2. **Real exported PDF pages** opened at full size, with the sample name readable. Sample name must be labeled as a sample so it is not mistaken for a real customer order.
3. **The real art, unretouched.** The 10 Pack 1 drawings (cow, pig, chicks, horse, sheep, duck, goat, barn, tractor, farmer) with their very thick outlines, shown full page, not thumbnails only.
4. **A real home print** of the PDF on a regular printer, US Letter, with the margin visible. Actual photo, not a mock-up.
5. **A real colored page**, colored by an actual child or by you with crayons/markers, honestly captioned. If no child has colored one yet (no parent feedback test done), show it colored by an adult and caption it that way, or leave this beat out.
6. **The spelling-confirmation message.** Only use a real thread if a real buyer allows it; otherwise use a clearly labeled sample. Never present a staged thread as a customer.
7. **Etsy's own AI-disclosure setting** left on in the listing (not a video beat, but must be true before publishing).

Not provable yet, so not shown: delivery time record, review screenshots, order counts, per-order timing. Time the first 3 real orders before even mentioning speed anywhere.

## Cut list

- Any "in this video" or "welcome back" opener; no logo sting longer than a blink.
- Any explanation of how AI line art is made, the prompts, or the generator tools (not what the gift buyer cares about; disclosure stays on the listing).
- The KDP book, the royalty math, the Harvest Time pack, and any cross-sell to other products. One product per video.
- Price, discount, "limited time," "launch special," or any urgency line. The research found no real urgency and an invented one hurts trust on a gift purchase.
- Reviews, star ratings, sales counts, "best seller," "trending," or "parent favorite," because none exist.
- A comparison against competitors' shops or any "unlike other sellers" line; it cannot be backed with evidence and invites a fight.
- Claims about learning, fine-motor skills, or development; the research found no support, and the offer dropped the learning angle.
- Turnaround stated as "fast" or "instant." Say "made after we confirm the spelling" instead; the 48-hour commitment lives in the listing text.
- The 15-minute-per-order figure and any labor/earnings talk (this is seller-side and audience-irrelevant).
- Party Set / multi-name tier ($24.99) until the offer's unlock rules are met.
- Fall/pumpkin visuals in the personalized cut, so it stays year-round and does not overlap with Harvest Time.
- Any stock footage of smiling kids; if a person is shown, it is a real hand, not stock.

## Open risks for this content

- All demand evidence for personalization is seller-side; the hook assumes the name is the purchase driver, which no buyer speech supports yet. If the first pins show views but no clicks, test the gift/occasion framing (birthday bag, party favor) against the name framing before changing the art.
- A new shop has no reviews, so beat 0:16-0:19 (spelling confirmation) carries the trust load. Do not paper over this with fake social proof.
- Pinterest working for coloring-book discovery comes from the workflow doc and is unverified here. Treat the first 30 days of pin impressions as the test, in line with the kill rule in 02-offer.md.
