# Free Cow Page Spec — "Free Farm Cow Page — Print Test"

Built from the lead magnet section of 04-conversion.md, Email 1 in 07-welcome-email.md and the art rules in 02-offer.md. This is one file, one page. It is GATED: don't build or publish it until both Etsy listings are live. Where this spec makes a choice that 04-conversion.md left open, it says so.

## Purpose

A test print. It answers one question before anyone buys: "Are these lines the right thickness for my kid?" It does not promise a free personalized page, claim kids will love it, or claim it beats free pages. Purchases happen on Etsy only.

## Page layout

1. The cow drawing, centered, filling the safe area inside the 0.5 in margins without touching them.
2. Footer, small, centered, inside the margin: `[Shop name] · Free farm cow page · For personal printing only · Line art is AI-generated`
3. Nothing else. No price, no Etsy or website link, no QR code, no "buy the full book" line, no logo larger than the footer text. The pitch lives in Emails 3 and 5, not on the page.

## Decision: the blank name banner

04-conversion.md defaulted to leaving a blank banner area so buyers see where the name would sit, and said to remove it if it reads as a free personalized page. This spec makes the opposite call: **no blank banner.** Reasons: Email 1 and the CTA both say "no name on it" and promise no free personalized page, and an empty name box can read as an unfinished or promised-personalized page. The name placement is shown in Email 3 and on the Etsy photos instead. If you'd rather keep the banner, you can add it back, but then say in the email that the space is where a name would go in the paid book. Confirm your choice before building.

## Build steps

1. Open the Pack 1 cow master (the version without the name banner) in Canva or the source file.
2. Place it on a US Letter page with 0.5 in margins, centered.
3. Add the footer text above in a small plain font.
4. Export as PDF (print quality) and flatten. Confirm the line art is black on white with no gray.
5. Name the file `farm-cow-page.pdf`. Store the editable source separately from the delivered PDF.

## QA checklist (run before it goes live)

1. Print on a home printer at 100% / Actual size, US Letter. The whole drawing and the footer are visible, nothing is cut off, and the lines are crisp and solid black, not gray or fuzzy.
2. Print again on A4 with "Fit to page". The whole page fits.
3. Hold it next to a printed Pack 1 page from the paid book. The outline thickness is identical.
4. Color a corner with chunky crayons and markers on plain paper to check how the lines hold. Report the result honestly (don't claim it prevents bleed-through).
5. Open it on a phone and on a computer. It opens, fits the screen and has no broken text.
6. Check the file's metadata and the footer text for typos and for any personal data.
7. Confirm the MailerLite delivery link or attachment works from a test sign-up on a clean email address.

## Delivery

- Delivered in Email 1 ("Your free farm cow page (print it first)") via MailerLite. Whether the PDF goes as a link or an attachment depends on MailerLite's free-plan options, which are unverified here. A hosted link is the safer default.
- The sign-up page lives on Pinterest only, never on Etsy. The page is for personal printing, which the footer says.

## What not to claim

- No "free personalized page", "your child's name", or "custom".
- No "kids love it", "keeps kids busy", "better than free pages", "best seller" or reviews.
- No "instant" language about the paid book.
- No learning or fine-motor claims.

## Verify before publishing (none confirmed here)

1. That the Pack 1 cow actually meets your own "very thick outlines" standard. No stroke width is specified in the research, so judge it by the print test above and keep it identical to the paid pages.
2. AI disclosure wording matches the Etsy listing and Etsy's current rules, and Pinterest's rules on AI content if the page is shown there.
3. Whether Etsy allows mentioning or promoting a free off-Etsy file in listing video or photos (see the compliance checklist in 04-conversion.md). The page itself carries no links, which keeps it safe either way.
4. CAN-SPAM items for Email 1: sender block, postal address, working unsubscribe link.
5. MailerLite free-plan limits, file size limits and whether it supports hosted downloads.
