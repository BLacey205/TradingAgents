# Revenue System — Personalized farm coloring book for young kids (Etsy printable, child's name on cover and every page)

## Summary

The buyer is a parent, grandparent or aunt/uncle shopping for a small gift or screen-free activity for a specific 3-6 year old who likes barns and tractors. The mechanism is a "Name-Swap Farm Master File": one Canva file where the child's name is typed once and lands on the cover, the "belongs to" page, a banner on each of 10 farm drawings and a certificate, then exported as a PDF after the spelling is confirmed by message. The core offer is "[Name]'s Farm Coloring Book" on Etsy, priced at $7.99 at launch and $9.99 later, with the existing Harvest Time pack as a cheap seasonal entry rung and a Farm Party Set locked until 10 stranger sales and a buyer asks for multiple names. The hook is "Free farm pages. Nobody's name on them.", delivered as a 22-25 second vertical video of a real name swap, cut for the Etsy listing, a Pinterest video pin and a 5-slide carousel. The funnel is Pinterest to a free generic cow "print test" page by email (MailerLite, gated until listings are live), then a 5-email sequence that ends in the Etsy listing, with all purchases staying on Etsy. Demand is not proven: the research verdict is weak-to-moderate, all evidence is seller-side, and the broad generic-farm pitch was dropped because competitors already sell it. The cheapest check that could overturn the whole system is a 10-minute logged-out Etsy search for "personalized farm coloring book" and "personalized coloring book with name", to be done before more art is made.

Unverified or invented figures to confirm before relying on them: all prices, the 48-hour turnaround, the 15-minute-per-order estimate, Etsy fee rates, MailerLite free-plan limits, and the Etsy/CAN-SPAM compliance items listed in section 4. The earlier CTA conflict ("from my shop" for a free page that lives off Etsy) is fixed: the CTA now points to Pinterest for the free page and Etsy for ordering. Still confirm Etsy allows mentioning an off-Etsy page in listing video (section 4).

## 2. Offer Architecture

# Offer Architecture — Personalized farm coloring book for young kids (Etsy printable, child's name on cover and pages)

Built on 01-research.md (second pass, 2026-10-01). Demand is NOT proven. Every demand point below is seller-side or tracker-side evidence, not buyer speech. Where this file leans on an inference, it says so.

## Positioning statement

For the parent, grandparent or aunt/uncle who needs a small gift or a screen-free activity for a specific 3-6 year old who loves barns and tractors: a farm coloring book that says "This book belongs to [Name]" on the cover and on every page, emailed to you within 48 hours so you can print it today and put it in a birthday bag, a farm-party favor or a basket.

Language note: phrases here ("belongs to," "name on the cover and throughout every page," "made to order," "screen-free," "quiet time") come from competitor listings and tags, not from buyers. Replace with real quotes after the Day 6 parent conversations.

## Unique mechanism

**The Name-Swap Farm Master File.** One locked Canva master file holds the farm pages already made in the AI workflow, each with a reserved blank banner space ("[Name]'s Farm") plus placeholders on the cover, the "belongs to" page and the certificate. Each order is: read the name from Etsy's personalization box, confirm the spelling by message, replace the name in the placeholders, export a PDF, upload it as the order file. Target is about 15 minutes per order; that is an estimate to be timed on the first 3 orders, not a measured figure.

Why it beats the half-solutions in the research:
- Free printable farm pages and $1.50-$3 generic packs have no name and no gift feel. The product differs in kind (a made-to-order gift), not in page count.
- Name coloring page generators produce bubble-letter name pages that the sources say need scenes around them to be a book. This puts the name into a finished farm book.
- Found personalized coloring books are princess, unicorn and calm themes. I found no farm one in search snippets. That gap is weak evidence (absence from snippets only); the Etsy search check below can overturn it.

What the mechanism does not do: it cannot run on KDP, and it does not scale past a few orders a day. That is acceptable for a one-person test.

## Core offer

- Deliverable: "[Name]'s Farm Coloring Book", a personalized printable PDF. 10 farm drawings from the existing set (use Pack 1 "Farm Favorites": cow, pig, chicks, horse, sheep, duck, goat, barn, tractor, farmer; very thick outlines, ages 3-6), plus a cover and "This book belongs to [Name]" page, and a banner with the child's name on each drawing page, and a certificate. Fall subjects stay in the Harvest Time pack so the two products do not overlap.
- Format: PDF, US Letter, 0.5 in margins, 300 DPI, one-sided for home printing. Etsy personalization box for the name, plus a message to confirm spelling. Etsy AI-disclosure option on. Note on the listing that A4 buyers print at 100% or "Fit to page."
- Timeframe: delivered within 48 hours of order (aim for 24). One free correction if the name was typed wrong by the seller; a spelling the buyer mistyped is fixed once on request.
- Price: **$7.99 at launch, move to $9.99 after the first real stranger reviews arrive.** This sits in the $7.99-$9.99 band that comparable personalized printable books charge (Payhip $7.99-$9.99, $9.99; $12.99 for a calm theme with the name on every page), against $1.50-$3 for generic packs.
  - Cost-of-inaction anchor, stated honestly: the research found the cost of inaction is weak. The buyer loses nothing material by skipping, since free pages exist. The only real trigger is a gift or occasion date (birthday, farm party, stocking, Easter basket) where instant delivery solves a last-minute need, and that trigger is plausible but unproven. So the price is not anchored to a pain figure. It is anchored to (a) the observed personalized-listing band and (b) being a fraction of a typical physical gift. I have no sourced figure for what a physical gift costs, so I do not state one. Expect this offer to sell on gift convenience, not on urgency.
  - Seller economics (fee rates are from memory, verify in Etsy's current fee page): at $7.99 about $0.52 transaction fee, about $0.49 payment processing, $0.20 listing, so roughly $6.80 net. At 15 minutes per order that is about $27/hour of labor. At $9.99 net is about $8.60. It is a side income, not a business, and the first stranger sale may take weeks.
- Startup cost: under $100, likely under $30. Free Canva and the existing art; Etsy listing fee about $0.20 per listing; optional ChatGPT $20; printer ink for one proof.

## Ladder

Each rung solves one named problem the previous rung did not.

1. Free: Pinterest preview pins and before/after flat-lay photos of the farm pages. Solves "where do I find farm pages at all." No money, no promise beyond showing the art. Evidence for Pinterest working for coloring books is only from the workflow doc's source, unverified here.
2. Lead magnet: one free generic farm page (the cow) in exchange for an email, via MailerLite's free plan. Solves "I'm not sure this art style is right for my kid" before buying anything. Defer until the listings are live (the workflow doc's own sequencing). No evidence yet that parents will trade an email for one page; treat as an experiment. It stays generic (no name) so it costs no manual work.
3. Entry rung, cheap seasonal: the existing "Harvest Time (Pack 3)" 13-page printable at about $2.99 (generic pack band $1.50-$3; the research saw a 20-page fall set at $2.99). Solves "I want something now for fall and want to test the art for pennies." List this week; fall pull fades by late November. Honest expectation: about $2 net per sale, a smoke detector for views and favorites, not income.
4. Mid-ticket, the core product: "[Name]'s Farm Coloring Book" at $7.99, then $9.99. Solves what the pack cannot: it is a gift made for one child, with the name in it. Page content is the same kind as rung 3; the new thing is the name and the gift framing, and that is the whole bet.
5. Premium, conditional: "Farm Party Set," the same book personalized for up to 6 children (party favors), about $24.99 (roughly $4 a child). Solves "I need this for a whole birthday party, not one kid," which the single book cannot. Do not build or list this until the single book has at least 10 strangers' sales and at least one buyer has asked for multiple names. No evidence yet for party demand; it is a hypothesis from the research's "farm-themed party" occasion. Done-for-you here means made-to-order by one person, so cap it at a few names.

Kept off the ladder on purpose: the generic 30-page KDP book ("Happy Farm Friends") is a separate track. It cannot be personalized (print-on-demand), its royalty math is weak (about $1.16 per $7.99 book per one 2026 guide, unconfirmed against KDP's calculator), and it should not be pitched as a step up from this offer. It is only worth continuing if the workflow doc's Test 3 passes. Do not cross-promote it as personalized.

## Evidence gaps and kill tests

- Do first, before more art: in a logged-out browser, search Etsy for "personalized farm coloring book" and "personalized coloring book with name." Record listing counts, prices, review counts and the top 3 sellers' review text. If farm-themed personalized listings are numerous and sell at $7.99-$9.99 with strong review counts, the gap is gone and this offer must differentiate harder or be dropped. If there are zero, that might mean an open niche or no demand; the check cannot tell which.
- Nothing in the research shows a buyer purchasing because of the name. The whole offer depends on that inference.
- Kill rule from the workflow doc (Test 2): under 50 views in 30 days is a kill, but a brand-new shop may fail on age alone, so check shop age and listing quality before concluding no demand. If views exist but no sales after 30 days, compare against the $7.99-$9.99 competitors first, then the preview images.
- Fulfillment kill: if orders take more than 20-25 minutes each by the third order, cut the per-page banner (name only on cover, belongs-to and certificate) before touching price.
- Handoff note for the content agent: voice must stay on gift/occasion and screen-free activity language; do not claim reviews, sales, or "best seller" status; keep the first Pinterest pins on Harvest Time for the seasonal window, and personalized pins year-round.
